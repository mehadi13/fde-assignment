Frequently Asked Questions

Traditional contractors work in isolation on assigned tickets. Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) operate as hybrid solutions architects and elite systems programmers who embed directly inside your engineering teams, attend standups, submit pull requests directly to your primary codebase, and own end-to-end production outcomes.

Because all our FDEs maintain active security background checks, pre-vetted cloud certifications, and standard NDA/IP templates, typical kickoff takes 48 to 72 hours from contract execution.

All Nexus FDE operations adhere strictly to SOC2 Type II, HIPAA, GDPR, and ISO 27001 standards. We support zero-trust access controls, hardware security keys, ephemeral access tokens, and air-gapped environment work.

You own 100% of the IP, source code, scripts, and documentation created during the engagement. Everything is committed directly into your repository with clear license headers and comprehensive documentation.

During the final phase of every engagement, our FDEs conduct structured technical walkthroughs, record video documentation, publish architectural specs, and mentor your internal engineering team to ensure total independence.
